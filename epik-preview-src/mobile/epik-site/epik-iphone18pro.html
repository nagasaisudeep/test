Product details

iPhone 18 Pro brings together Apple's most advanced chip, a redesigned camera system, and a display built for the way you actually use your phone. Powered by the A20 Pro chip built on a 2nm process, it delivers meaningfully faster performance and a more efficient GPU, while a triple 48MP Pro Fusion camera system, headlined by a genuinely new variable aperture main camera, gives you real creative control over every shot. A refined aluminum unibody design, a redesigned Dynamic Island, and Apple's next generation Apple Intelligence tools round out what is Apple's biggest Pro upgrade in several years.

Key features

  • A20 Pro Chip Built on a 2nm process, with a 6-core CPU, 7-core GPU with Neural Accelerators, and a dual 16-core Neural Engine, up to 40% faster GPU performance and 50% more memory bandwidth than the A19 Pro
  • 48MP Pro Fusion Camera System Variable aperture main camera (f/1.4 to f/4.0), giving genuine creative control over depth of field and light, alongside a 48MP Ultra Wide camera with macro photography and a 48MP Periscope Telephoto camera with 5x optical zoom
  • Pro Camera Controls Adjustable aperture, shutter speed, white balance, and a live histogram, alongside Adaptive True Tone flash and Portrait Lighting with six effects
  • 6.3 Inch Super Retina XDR Display 120Hz ProMotion, LTPO+, HDR, True Tone, and up to 3,000 nits peak outdoor brightness
  • Refined Aluminum Unibody Design Redesigned Dynamic Island, larger vapor chamber for improved cooling, and Ceramic Shield 2 protection
  • Next Generation Apple Intelligence Integrated across apps and built with privacy at the core, including an all new version of Siri
  • Apple C2 Modem with Apple N1 Networking Chip Supporting Satellite Emergency SOS with full internet support and Ultra Wideband (UWB 3)
  • Up to 34 Hours of Video Playback A genuine improvement in everyday battery life
  • MagSafe and Qi2 Wireless Charging Up to 25W each
  • IP68 Water and Dust Resistance For genuine everyday durability
  • Environmentally Conscious Manufacturing 50% of manufacturing electricity for this generation sourced from renewable energy, and zero landfill waste from final assembly sites under Apple's Zero Waste Programme
